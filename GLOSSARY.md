# Career Guide Skills glossary

These terms describe how readers use the workbook and directory to take the next step in a career.

## Language

**Career guide book**

A self-paced workbook that combines instruction, practical work, and reusable worksheets for a career goal.

**Milestone**

A stage in a **Career guide book** with a specific outcome and a **Readiness checkpoint**.

**Readiness checkpoint**

Observable evidence a reader can use to decide whether to advance, repeat a task, or change direction.

**Reusable worksheet**

A standalone, photocopy-ready page for recording the same type of work on multiple occasions.

**Source register**

A dated list of evidence linked to the claims or listings it supports.

**Local opportunity directory**

A geographically scoped collection of public **Leads** and **Confirmed openings** relevant to a career goal.

**Lead**

An organization or public business contact worth researching or contacting for a stated reason.

**Confirmed opening**

An opportunity with explicit, current evidence that applications or inquiries are being accepted.
