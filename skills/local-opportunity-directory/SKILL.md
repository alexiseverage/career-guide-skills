# Local opportunity directory

Produce a dated directory the reader can use to prioritize outreach. Default to a **print-ready PDF and self-contained editable HTML**. Let the number and density of relevant listings determine the length unless the user requests a page budget.

1. Identify the career goal, listing types, and geographic scope. If a radius is requested, establish a public origin, radius, and units. Use a town center or other public reference point to protect the privacy of the reader. A radius defaults to straight-line distance. Apply a route distance or travel time only when the user asks for that measure. Offer the optional aesthetic in the same intake and follow [aesthetic integration](references/aesthetic.md).
2. Apply [search and verification](references/search.md) to locate candidates, confirm public premises and contacts, check geography, and deduplicate. Use public professional contact channels and dated evidence. When web access is unavailable or the reader requests no browsing, use supplied evidence with its scope and as-of date clearly labeled, or provide a blank research worksheet.
3. Apply [listing structure](references/listings.md) to rank the results, show why each listing is relevant, and distinguish a lead from a confirmed opening. Explain gaps and state the method, coverage, and date checked. Include a concrete outreach step or reusable tracker where useful. Draft outreach without contacting anybody unless requested.
4. Follow [print and delivery](references/print.md) to build the source and PDF. Check page breaks, legibility, contact information, source links, and any requested page limit. If rendering or inspection is unavailable, deliver the source or structured listings and identify the pending checks.
