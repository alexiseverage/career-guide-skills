Milestone 1 · Lesson

Record the condition before touching a tool

A short intake record helps a mechanic separate what they observed from what still needs a closer check. Write a physical observation and record where you saw it.

Worked example

“The rear tread has a visible cut near the centerline. I marked its location in my photo. A mechanic should assess it before the bicycle is ridden.”

What to practice next

Record three observations using the same approach. Start with a stationary bicycle and photograph only equipment you have permission to document.

Milestone 1 · Action

Complete a practice inspection

  • Record the date and a label you can use to identify the bicycle later.
  • Write three observations, with the relevant location on the bicycle for each.
  • Circle one item you would ask a mechanic to assess.

Observations

Evidence to save

Readiness checkpoint

Ask a second reader to locate the three observations from your notes. If feedback is pending, compare and refine a second set of notes using the practice log.
